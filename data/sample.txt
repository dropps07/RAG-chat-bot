Green Valley Public Library — Visitor Guide

The Green Valley Public Library is located at 12 River Road, Green Valley. It was founded in 1987 and is open to all residents of Green Valley free of charge. The library holds more than 45,000 books, including fiction, non-fiction, reference books and a large collection for children.

Opening hours are Monday to Friday from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm, and Saturday from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm. The library is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. The reading room on the first floor stays open until 7:00 pm on weekdays for students preparing for exams.

Membership is free for residents of Green Valley. To join, bring a photo ID and proof of address to the front desk. Visitors from outside Green Valley can buy a temporary membership for 100 rupees per month. Members may borrow up to five books at a time for fourteen days. Books can be renewed once, for another seven days, if no one else has reserved them.

The late fee is 10 rupees per book per day. If a book is lost or damaged, the member must pay the cost of replacing it. The children's section is on the ground floor and has picture books, story books and a small play area. Children under the age of ten must be accompanied by an adult.

The library offers free Wi-Fi to all members. There are twelve computers in the study hall that members can use for up to one hour per day. Printing costs 5 rupees per page and photocopying costs 2 rupees per page.

Every first Saturday of the month, the library hosts a reading club at 11:00 am in the community hall. The club is open to all members and there is no fee to join. The head librarian is Mrs. Sunita Rao. She can be reached at the front desk or by phone on 011-4567-8901.

The library also runs a summer reading programme for school children during May and June. Children who finish five books during the programme receive a certificate. Last year, 240 children took part in the programme.

The study hall on the second floor has seating for eighty students and is kept silent at all times. Students must show their membership card at the entrance to the study hall. Food and drinks are not allowed inside the study hall, but there is a small canteen on the ground floor that serves tea, coffee and snacks from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm.

Every year in December, the library holds a book fair in the community hall. Publishers from across the country set up stalls, and members get a ten percent discount on all purchases. The book fair runs for three days, from Friday to Sunday, and entry is free for members. Last December, over 3,000 people visited the fair.

The library's digital section offers e-books and audiobooks. Members can borrow up to three e-books at a time through the library's mobile app. The app is free to download and works on Android and iPhone. For help with the app, ask at the front desk or email help@greenvalleylibrary.in.
